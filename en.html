A custom world

This isn't the map you remember with a few extras bolted on. The world itself has been rebuilt: reshaped terrain, new landmarks, and towns and zones that exist nowhere else.

  • The Great Desert has swallowed the Blasted Lands: a huge new level 60 zone of sand worms, a crashed Exodar, a Titan gate and ruins from another world.
  • Tanaris waits at the end of the road: a level 59-60 elite finale where the Caverns of Time have shattered across the desert and the ocean is falling into the world.
  • The Ruins of Alterac have risen again as a walled city under a mysterious new ruler, with a weekly elite boss for both factions.
  • Uldum and the Twilight Highlands are part of the world, each with its own story.
  • Silithus, Arathi, Stranglethorn, Hillsbrad, Dustwallow, Winterspring, Stonetalon, Desolace and more have been rebuilt with new quests, new towns and new threats.

Cities that live

Stormwind and Orgrimmar have hundreds of residents and over 140 city quests. Guards react to your reputation, bounty boards hang in the inns, and a train loops through Orgrimmar and the valley. In Rocketzan, the goblin capital, rival families fight over the market.

A story of its own

Ley lines are cracking open across Kalimdor, a king sits on Lordaeron's throne, and the Forsaken are torn between those who want peace and those who don't. You aren't the chosen one here. You're one adventurer among many, in a world that keeps moving with or without you.

Be someone new

Races you can't play anywhere else

Alongside the classic races, play as a Murloc, a Tuskarr, a Worgen or a Goblin, with full gear support and race and class combinations the original game never allowed.

The Necromancer

Our own class. Raise the dead, curse your enemies with plague and blood, and learn your darkest spells from books hidden across the world. Its story begins long ago, in a village called Vandermar.

Every class, reworked

New talent trees and abilities for every class, from the warrior's Heroic Leap and Avatar to the shaman's Lava Surge and a priest's Lightwell that fights on its own. And a profession for every class: hunters fletch, shamans carve totems, warriors learn shouts in an ancient tongue, warlocks bind souls, priests keep devotions, paladins hunt relics, druids tend the wild and necromancers work bone.

Adventure that bites back

Every dungeon from level 17 to 60 has been reworked, each with its own story, new bosses and new mechanics. They're meant to be a challenge.

  • Shadowfang Keep: an apothecary comes to buy the worgen curse, and Arugal is a new multi-phase fight.
  • Scarlet Monastery: the Crusade defies Uther, with a new finale in the Cathedral.
  • Dire Maul: a ley line wakes beneath Eldre'Thalas, and all 20 bosses are reworked.
  • Stratholme: the Grave Tithe, and the Baron Run works again.

Raids and more

Upper Blackrock Spire as a 10-player raid, the Zandalari city in Stranglethorn, and Karazhan as the first raid at level 60. Between dungeons, hunt 42 hidden cave mini-dungeons and open-world dungeons like House Barlow.

Loot worth chasing

World-drop epic sets at levels 20, 40, 50 and 60: Bind to Account, no class restrictions, and every set one matching outfit. Dungeon Fragments and Cores craft into new armour sets, and 55 Realm Firsts wait for those who want to be first.

Full PvP zones

Out in the open world, some lands belong to whoever can hold them. In Revelation's full PvP zones, everyone is fair game, and the rewards are worth the risk.

  • The Great Desert: a level 60 war zone where Horde and Alliance fight over capture points at the Oasis, the Crystal Field and the Titan Gate, while a roaming elite boss hunts both sides.
  • Rich ground: plenty of gathering nodes, hard mobs, and caves that pay out double materials.
  • Eastern Feralas: a high-level PvP zone where the gear drops are worth fighting over.

Prefer to stay safe? Most of the world is yours to explore in peace. PvP is there when you want it.

Look the part

Your look is yours. Transmogrification lets you wear any appearance you've collected, and your collection is shared across your whole account.

And you earn it by playing, not by paying: the looks worth chasing aren't locked behind your card. They drop in dungeons, come from rare world-drop recipes, wait at the end of quests and turn up out in the world.

  • 330 outfits crafted from rare world-drop recipes.
  • Five wild colourways of every store mount, found only out in the world: from gathering, from themed dungeons, and a few that hatch from eggs.
  • Wings, emotes, flags and around 50 dances to make your character stand out.

A life beyond combat

  • Professions rebuilt from 1 to 315: Blacksmithing, Tailoring and Leatherworking with rare world-drop plans and signature pieces that turn epic, and new metals to mine.
  • Your own home: over 1,000 apartments in Stormwind, furnished with what you build in Construction, the housing profession.
  • Farming: grow crops out in the world and cook over 200 new dishes.
  • Pet battles: collect, level and battle your companions out in the world.
  • A Pick Pocket career for rogues: stolen goods, fences, city bounties and two rival thieves' guilds.
  • The Rocketzan casino: blackjack, slots, Rocket Crash and Texas Hold'em, for in-game gold only.

Easy to start, easy to come back

  • Free to play, with nothing in the way between you and the whole game.
  • One launcher downloads the game, keeps it up to date and repairs it for you.
  • An HD client with a new interface, longer view distance and much better performance.

  • The Ancestral Vault: an account-wide bank shared by all your characters.
  • Over 1,300 quests show you where to go on the map.

Free to play. Fair for everyone.

Revelation is free to play. Our shop sells cosmetics only: custom emotes, dances, flags and other ways to show off. Nothing in it gives you power, an edge or a shortcut, so every player stands on the same ground. Even your transmog isn't gated behind your wallet: the gear and looks are found out in the world.

Prices are kept low on purpose. The shop is there so players who want to give something back can help keep the realm running. We don't do this for the money. We do it for the players, and the years this server has stood are the proof.
